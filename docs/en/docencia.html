How I work

Four ways
to work together.

Masterclass

Up to 3 h

Lecture with a live demo. Ideal as a one-off activity, event day or forum.

Seminar

3 to 6 h

Applied theory and guided practice with production files. Every student leaves with a deliverable.

Hands-on lab

3 h

Students build the rig from scratch, step by step, with a complete reference rig as a guide.

Tailored

As needed

Several seminars combined, or content designed with the academic coordinator for the student profile.

FormatIn person in Madrid, in the school's classroom with one workstation per student, or live online on the school's platform (Spain and Latin America).
FitAdapted to the group's level and designed to complement the school's programme, not replace it. Materials and working files included.
